Ooty

Queen of Hill Stations

Ooty is a beautiful hill station located in Tamil Nadu. It is famous for its cool climate, tea gardens, green hills, and peaceful natural lakes.

✨ Famous Places in Ooty

🌊 Ooty Lake

A serene artificial lake surrounded by eucalyptus trees with boating facilities.

Sprawling lush green garden home to exotic flower species and trees.
